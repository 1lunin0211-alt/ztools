SWTool 1.1 (2026)

Localized build based on the original Chinese ZTool v3.84 package
(full version history of ZTool 1.0 – 3.84, 2017 – 2026, is archived
by the vendor).

Changes relative to ZTool 3.84:
1. Complete UI translation into Russian and English.
2. The built-in auto-updater has been replaced by the new licensing
   server https://license.vizbuka.ru/ztool.
3. Per-licence-key binding (online activation, 7-day offline grace,
   licence transfer between devices).
4. Demo mode: 3 minutes of work without activation. When it expires
   the ribbon operations are locked and a notice is shown, after which
   the application closes after 10 seconds (matches the original).
5. Built-in links to obsolete third-party services have been removed
   (z-tool.cn, the QQ support group).
